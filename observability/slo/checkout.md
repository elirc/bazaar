# Checkout Success SLO

- Objective: 99.5% of server-authoritative checkout attempts succeed over 30 days.
- Page: five-minute success ratio below 98% with at least 100 attempts.
- Ticket: one-hour success ratio below 99.2% with at least 100 attempts.
- Exclude explicit buyer declines; include inventory/order/provider system failures.
- Page links: checkout trace search, payment outage, inventory drift, rollback runbooks.
